# SIRINX AI Co-worker OS Content System

Status: local-only content planning system

## Content Pillars

1. Problem awareness
   - You use AI, but still do everything yourself.
   - AI helps 24/7, but without workflow it becomes scattered work.
   - The result is tired owners, messy work, and no time for strategy.

2. System education
   - What is an AI co-worker?
   - Why AI needs roles, context, workflow, and approval.
   - How an AI team differs from a chatbot.

3. Module education
   - Marketing Co-worker
   - Content Co-worker
   - Admin Co-worker
   - Finance Co-worker
   - Video Co-worker
   - Workflow Design Co-worker

4. Transformation
   - From prompt to team.
   - From scattered work to operating system.
   - From manual repetitive work to review-based workflow.

5. Safety and trust
   - Human approval gates.
   - Local-first setup.
   - Evidence packets.
   - No real secrets in prototypes.

## 8-Second Video Formula

```text
0-2s: name the pain or promise
2-5s: show co-worker department or workflow
5-8s: show dashboard/CTA and one clear benefit
```

## Voiceover Rule

Use Thai voiceover for long meaning. Keep on-screen Thai short and readable.

## Example Voiceover

```text
ถ้าคุณใช้ AI อยู่ แต่ยังต้องคิดเอง เขียนเอง และสรุปเองทุกวัน
แปลว่ายังไม่ได้วาง AI เป็นระบบงานของธุรกิจ
SIRINX AI Co-worker OS ช่วยเปลี่ยน AI ให้เป็นทีมงานที่ทำงานเป็นขั้นตอน และตรวจสอบได้ค่ะ
```

## Negative Prompt / Avoid List

- no fake metrics
- no unreadable Thai text
- no overfilled dashboard
- no guaranteed revenue claim
- no "AI replaces every employee" claim
- no platform abuse language
- no private customer data
- no official partnership badge unless verified
