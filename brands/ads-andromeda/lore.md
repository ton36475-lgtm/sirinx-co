# Andromeda Lore

Status: local-only brand lore
Owner: SIRINXDev
Brand: ADS ANDROMEDA

This file defines the fictional brand universe for Andromeda. It exists to make
the character repeatable, memorable, and useful for educational short-form
content.

No factual platform partnership, guarantee, or operational claim is created by
this lore.

## Universe

```yaml
universe: ADS ANDROMEDA
location: Andromeda Command Center

mission:
  - help marketers understand advertising systems
  - explain risk-aware ad infrastructure in simple language
  - make account, page, BM, and campaign structure easier to understand

specialty:
  - Facebook
  - Meta
  - Marketing Infrastructure
  - Account Quality
  - Business Manager
  - Page Strategy
  - Risk Management

tagline:
  - Grow Smarter
  - Scale Better
```

## Character Role In The Universe

Andromeda is the AI Marketing Commander inside the Andromeda Command Center.
She does not hard-sell first. She scans the marketing system, explains the
simple lesson, then points the viewer toward a safer next step.

## Narrative Formula

```text
Misunderstanding
  -> Andromeda clarifies the system
  -> one practical rule
  -> soft ADS ANDROMEDA brand cue
```

## Lore Boundaries

- This universe is a brand storytelling device.
- Do not imply Andromeda is a real official Meta/Facebook representative.
- Do not imply ADS ANDROMEDA controls platform policy.
- Do not imply guaranteed results or guaranteed account survival.
- Keep lore practical, short, and tied to marketing education.

## Reusable Lore Prompt

```text
Set the scene inside the Andromeda Command Center, a purple-blue galaxy
advertising operations room. Andromeda, the AI Marketing Commander, explains one
marketing infrastructure lesson with calm authority. Use hologram panels, clean
icons, and a soft ADS ANDROMEDA brand cue. The tone is educational first and
```
