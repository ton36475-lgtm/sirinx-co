# Proof And Case Study System

Status: proof engine.

## Proof Types

- before/after visuals
- reach and engagement screenshots
- lead generated
- reply generated
- customer testimonial
- delivery speed
- reusable prompt/template created
- client outcome narrative

## Case Study Template

1. Client / niche.
2. Problem.
3. Offer used.
4. Deliverables.
5. Before state.
6. After state.
7. Metrics.
8. Screenshots/assets.
9. Testimonial.
10. Next offer.

## Full Auto Actions

- auto-draft case studies from delivery records
- auto-request proof checklist
- auto-create before/after story outline
- auto-update proof tracker

Blocked:

- fabricated metrics
- unverified testimonials
- private client data in public case studies
