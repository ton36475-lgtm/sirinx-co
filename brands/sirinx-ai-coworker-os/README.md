# SIRINX AI Co-worker OS

Status: local-only brand and product operating spec
Owner: SIRINXDev / GhostClaw
Proof status: LOCAL

SIRINX AI Co-worker OS is the SME-facing product lane for turning AI from a
chat/prompt tool into a governed digital team.

Core promise:

```text
Not just prompts.
An AI team that understands the business, follows workflows, reports progress,
and helps the owner make decisions with evidence.
```

## Source Intake

This package is extracted from the 2026-06-07 visual set:

- Roadmap: 6 implementation modules plus bonus video co-worker.
- Problem: SMEs use AI but still do thinking, writing, summaries, reporting,
  finance checks, and video work manually because AI is not yet a business
  operating system.
- Position: AI co-workers should be installed as a team with roles, context,
  workflows, evidence, and approval gates.
