# 90-Day Scale Plan

Status: scale sprint.

## Days 1-30

Validate the AI Local Promo Pack and collect first proof.

## Days 31-60

Turn repeatable work into:

- monthly retainer
- prompt/template product
- referral system
- delivery SOP
- proof dashboard

## Days 61-90

Scale with:

- AI Content Agency offer
- AI Automation Setup offer
- owned audience
- newsletter
- sponsor/content packages
- partner workflows

## Scale Rule

Do not scale a channel until:

- offer is clear
- delivery SOP exists
- proof exists
- metrics are tracked
- opt-in and rate limits are configured
- budget cap exists
