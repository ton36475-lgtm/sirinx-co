# Andromeda Character Bible

Status: locked-local-character-standard
Owner: SIRINXDev
Brand: ADS ANDROMEDA

Andromeda is the recurring educational character for ADS ANDROMEDA. Her job is
to teach short, practical advertising infrastructure lessons before any direct
offer appears.

## Identity

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Name | Andromeda |
| Role | AI Marketing Commander |
| Brand function | Educational presenter and risk-aware marketing guide |
| Core promise | Teach first, sell second |
| Main format | 6-8 second vertical short clips |

## Personality

- Intelligent.
- Polite.
- Professional.
- Concise.
- Calm under pressure.
- Helpful without overclaiming.
- Risk-aware before promotional.

## Visual Appearance

| Field | Direction |
|---|---|
| Character | Futuristic Thai woman |
| Wardrobe | Black cyber-luxury executive suit |
| Color mood | Purple-blue galaxy, electric cyan, deep navy |
| Environment | Advertising command room / galaxy command center |
| Camera | Slow cinematic movement, clean portrait framing |
| Gesture | Small confident gestures, no exaggerated acting |

## Speaking Rules

- Use Thai voiceover.
- Keep lines short and useful.
- One lesson per clip.
- Avoid long Thai text on screen.
- Use icons and short English labels for support.
- No pressure-selling, fear-selling, or fake urgency.

## Compliance Rules

- Do not imply official Meta/Facebook partnership.
- Do not promise account approval, ad approval, ban-proof operation, platform
  immunity, or guaranteed results.
- Do not show real customer data, credentials, account IDs, tokens, or backend
  screens.
- Do not position the character as legal, financial, or platform-policy
  authority.

## Content Ratio

| Ratio | Content Type | Examples |
|---:|---|---|
| 70% | Knowledge | Account quality, BM basics, page age, risk management |
| 20% | Product review | Facebook Page 2021, BM3 Verify, inventory explainers |
| 10% | Direct offer | New stock, promotion, limited campaign |
