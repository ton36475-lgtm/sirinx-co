# Delivery SOP

Status: delivery lane.

## Client Delivery Flow

1. Intake / onboarding form.
2. Brief confirmation.
3. Asset collection.
4. Production board.
5. Draft output.
6. QA checklist.
7. Client delivery.
8. Revision cycle.
9. Final package.
10. Testimonial request.
11. Case-study conversion.

## Delivery Checklist

- scope matches offer
- file names are clean
- captions are platform-ready
- prompts are reusable
- visuals have clear direction
- customer notes are logged
- revision limit is tracked
- proof request is prepared

## Full Auto Delivery

The system can auto-generate drafts, checklists, package indexes, and status
updates. External customer messaging requires opt-in and approved channel rules
from policy.
