# Andromeda Marketing Tips - Master Frame

Status: local-only series standard
Owner: SIRINXDev
Depends on: `andromeda-character-bible.md`, `design.md`, `voice.md`

This file is the reusable master frame for short ADS ANDROMEDA educational
clips.

## Master Frame

```yaml
series_name: Andromeda Marketing Tips
duration: 8s
aspect_ratio: 9:16

character:
  name: Andromeda
  role: AI Marketing Commander

style:
  - luxury cyberpunk
  - galaxy command center
  - premium enterprise

camera:
  - slow cinematic movement
  - clean portrait framing
  - minimal movement, high clarity

text:
  rule: minimal
  allowed:
    - icons
    - short English labels
    - numbers
    - ADS ANDROMEDA logo

voice:
  language: Thai
  voice: Thai female
  tone:
    - confident
    - friendly
    - professional

goal:
  primary: educate first
  secondary: sell second
```

## Reusable 8-Second Structure

| Segment | Time | Purpose | Pattern |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Hook | 0-2s | Introduce one question or misconception | Andromeda appears beside hologram |
| Explain | 2-5s | Give 2-3 criteria or concept points | Icons or short labels appear |
| Close | 5-8s | Give safe takeaway and soft brand cue | Andromeda faces camera |

## Default Visual Layout

```text
Top safe area:
  small episode title or logo

Middle:
  Andromeda presenter
  one hologram panel with 2-3 icons

Bottom safe area:
  minimal CTA or brand lockup
```

## Reusable Icon Set

- Account Quality
- Usage History
- Support
- Page Age

- Business Manager
- Team Access
- Risk Control

## Quality Gates

- One idea per clip.
- 8 seconds maximum unless specifically approved.
- No long Thai overlay.
- Voiceover must match the lesson exactly.
- Product reference appears only if the episode is in the 20% product or 10%
  offer category.
- Every claim must be supportable by `brand.md` and `campaign-offers.md`.
