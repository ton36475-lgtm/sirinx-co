# SIRINX AI Co-worker OS Brand

## Brand

Name: SIRINX AI Co-worker OS
Powered by: GhostClaw x SIRINXDev
Category: AI business operating system for SMEs and new teams

## Positioning

SIRINX AI Co-worker OS helps SMEs install AI as a working digital team, not just
as a chatbot. The system combines co-worker roles, business context, workflow
templates, approval gates, reports, and content/video production workflows.

## Audience

- SME owners
- Solo founders
- Small marketing teams
- Agencies building repeatable AI workflows
- Operators who already use AI but still carry all execution work manually

## Core Message

```text
Use AI as a team, not just a prompt box.
```

Thai message:

```text
เปลี่ยน AI จากเครื่องมือตอบคำถาม ให้เป็นทีมงานดิจิทัลของธุรกิจคุณ
```

## Personality

- Practical
- Premium but not distant
- Business-focused
- Safe and governed
- Clear, structured, outcome-oriented

## Visual Language

- Dark command center
- Black / deep navy base
- Electric blue and cyan highlights
- Purple accent for marketing/video co-workers
- Clean dashboard UI
- Friendly robot co-workers
- Neon-but-readable information cards

## Voice

Thai primary, English support.

Tone:

- Direct
- Helpful
- Operator-level
- No hype without evidence
- No impossible guarantee

## Avoid

- Claiming AI replaces all staff
- Claiming guaranteed revenue
- Claiming zero risk
- Using fake customer results
- Using private customer data in examples
- Overcrowded posters
- Long Thai text on video frames
- Confusing AI autonomy with uncontrolled automation

## Approved Claims

- Helps reduce repetitive work when configured correctly.
- Helps make workflows easier to see and repeat.
- Helps create draft content, reports, dashboards, and video plans.
- Works best with human review and approval gates.
- Can be installed locally first before any external activation.

## Claims Requiring Evidence Before Use

- Time saved percentages
- Revenue impact percentages
- Cost reduction percentages
- Customer outcomes
- Automation coverage percentages
- 24/7 production support claims
