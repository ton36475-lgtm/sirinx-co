# Autonomous Marketing Policy

Status: active design layer.

## Allowed Automatically

- Create content calendars.
- Draft captions, scripts, newsletters, lead magnets, and landing copy.
- Schedule or publish only to owned whitelisted channels when rate limits pass.
- Send email/LINE/customer messages only to opt-in contacts.
- Create CRM records from opt-in forms or owned inbound channels.
- Generate daily performance reports.

## Blocked Automatically

- Spam or outreach to non-opt-in contacts.
- LinkedIn automation using personal browser sessions or credentials.
- Scraping contact data that violates platform terms or privacy policy.
- Paid ad spend beyond budget caps.
- Sensitive news/legal/political publish without a safe category rule.
- Any action that hides sender identity, missing unsubscribe, or missing opt-out.

## Project Defaults

- SIRINX Solar: opt-in lead follow-up and quote drafts only.
- AGM AUTOGLOW: owned-channel creative content and export packs.
- Phitsanulok United News: low-risk content categories only; sensitive items quarantine.
- Kusala: high-trust scripts and internal case routing; external messages must be opt-in and template-bound.
